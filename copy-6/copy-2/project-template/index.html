Antitekt:in

ZHdK Bachelor Game Design 
May 2025

brief

Create a board game in a group of two.

Concept

Antitekt:in is a boardgame for 3 – 8 players. It strikes through its simplicity, the easy way to learn the rules. The game could be categorized as an "Imposter Game", where the group has to find the one person pulling strings. The game is about building a tower together as architects, meanwhile the "Antitect" tries to bring the tower to fall down through deception. Its known for creating fun and chaos in the group.
