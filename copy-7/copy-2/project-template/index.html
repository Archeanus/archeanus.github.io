brief

Create a video in the software After Effects. The video has to be a music video for a song you like. Its about motion and the dynamic how the music gets translated into motion.

Concept

I chose the track "Demeter" by the German artist Schrotthagen. Inspired by the title of this techno track, I built my visuals around the divine side of Greek mythology. The Greek statue and the all-seeing eye both allude to the goddess Demeter. I paid close attention to the beats and audio effects so that the viewer feels fully immersed, both visually and aurally. The video is envisioned as a VJ-style piece, meant to be projected behind a DJ on an LED wall.
